What is the difference between Birth Number (Mulank) and Destiny Number (Bhagyank)?

Your Birth Number (Mulank) is derived from your single day of birth and governs your core personality, internal nature, and primary behavior. Your Destiny Number (Bhagyank) is calculated from the complete sum of your day, month, and year of birth, reflecting your life path, career calling, and karmic destiny.
